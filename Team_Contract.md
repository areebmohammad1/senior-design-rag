## 5. Decision-Making Framework & Tiebreaker
The team adheres to a structured four-step decision flow: **Propose $\to$ Discuss $\to$ Decide $\to$ Document**.
1. **Proposal:** Any member may propose an architectural change, scope adjustment, or tooling decision with supporting rationale.  
2. **Discussion:** The team reviews technical trade-offs, implementation overhead, and project constraints.  
3. **Consensus:** Decisions are reached by mutual agreement whenever possible.  
4. **Tiebreaker:** If a technical impasse occurs, the Lead Developer (Mohammad Areeb) serves as internal tiebreaker. Persistent unresolved questions are escalated to Dr. Curtis-Trudel.  
5. **Documentation:** Key decisions and architectural rationales are recorded in the project repository.
